HeliOS · warehouse operating system

An operating system, not middleware.

HeliOS runs the whole warehouse from one place: planning, scaling, flow, people, costs, maintenance and the automation itself. Built by engineers, made for the operators and ground team who run the site every day.

Not a WCS bolt-on

Most warehouse control software is custom middleware, built for one site and changed only by specialists. HeliOS is a product, the same on every site, configured by the operator without code.

AI built on engineering knowledge

The AI works from Orion's own knowledge base: the faults, drawings, maintenance history and site experience of the team that builds the machines. It supports the team; the experience is Orion's.

Runs Orion and third-party kit

Native on Helix, and connects to Siemens and Beckhoff PLCs, Interroll conveyors and AMR fleets. Live at APC Overnight's Cannock hub, controlling the Jupiter line.

About Orion MIS

Boilerplate, ready to paste.

Orion MIS is a British technology house based in West Sussex. Founded in 2019, the company designs, builds, codes and supports its own warehouse automation, including the Helix parcel sorter and HeliOS control software. Helix steers parcels on pivoting wheels with 200 mm gaps between items; at APC Overnight's Cannock hub, the Jupiter line peaks at 13,000 items an hour on mixed freight. Orion employs 40 people and offers its systems to buy outright, or to pay for monthly or per parcel.
