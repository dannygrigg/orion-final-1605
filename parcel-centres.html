FAQs

Parcel sortation, asked directly.

The questions parcel centre operators actually ask before signing scope.

What throughput can Helix actually deliver in my building?

Helix is sized to your destination count, parcel mix and shift pattern. Live installations run between 4,000 and 20,000 parcels per hour, with the upper end driven by additional sorter slices and faster belt speeds. We model realistic throughput for your operation as part of the site survey — not a brochure number.

Can Helix handle awkward parcels?

Yes. Helix is designed for real parcel environments — bags, boxes, mixed sizes, polybags, uneven loads and high-volume peaks. Anything outside the standard envelope is profiled in our UK facility before specification — we run sample parcels through a live Helix before signing scope.

How modular is Helix really — can we add destinations later?

Yes. Helix is built around 200mm cassette modules. Adding sort destinations later means extending the divert run and adding cages, not rebuilding the system. Customers regularly start with 16–24 destinations and expand as volume grows.

How much does a parcel sortation system cost?

Cost depends on throughput, destinations, parcel profile, WCS scope, integration scope and install conditions. All Orion automation is available on monthly finance, with entry systems typically from £4,000/month on a 60-month term, subject to specification and finance approval. The ROI calculator gives a sized estimate; send us your numbers and we'll come back with a formal proposal.
