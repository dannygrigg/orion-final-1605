F-01 · fault

Belt conveyor drive stations — motor mounting, drive-drum shaft and bearings

In progressNext · Design · 13 Nov 2026
Location
Every belt conveyor drive station
First reported
23 Oct 2026
What happens
Toothed belt lost tension and alignment and sheared teeth, stopping the conveyor on 23 Oct at 20:45. Further drive-station failures in November. A 20 mm drum shaft measured worn to 16.5 mm in its bearing on 6 Nov, with the bearings not positively located on the shaft.
Interim measure
Stations checked and re-tensioned at each attendance; toothed belts and fasteners held on site.
StageStatusOwnerTargetCompleteFinding · measure · notes
InvestigationCompleteD. Grigg—26 Oct 2026Orion report ORI-APC-RCA-001 issued 26 Oct. Site assessment by D. Grigg and C. Morgan on 10 Nov: the drive stations as supplied are not fit for retrofit.
Root causeCompleteD. Grigg—10 Nov 2026Motor located by friction in slotted holes, so start torque rotates it and slackens the belt. Drum shaft and bearing arrangement does not seat both bearings, so the shaft turns in the race and wears.
DesignIn progressD. Grigg13 Nov 2026—New Orion drive station: drive-drum roller with bearings housed outside the frame, 25 mm shaft seating both bearings, toothed belt tensioned by a concentric adjuster. Design review Fri 13 Nov; released for manufacture on approval.
ImplementationNot startedOrion site teamTo be confirmed—Orion fabricates the stations and retrofits every belt drive station in a single weekend changeover with testing before hand-back. Drum rollers manufactured by AED Rollers; their lead time sets the date, which Orion will request from APC once the design is released.
VerificationNot startedOrion / APC——Checked in service, run-in, then signed off by APC.

F-02 · fault

Poly-V driven rollers — roller ends pulling out, bands jumping, rollers lifting

In progressNext · Implementation
Location
All poly-V driven sections; skew conveyor worst affected
First reported
30 Jul 2026
What happens
Roller end inserts pull out of the tube in running, drive bands fray and climb off the heads, and rollers lift out of their frame location. The failures recurred after the original manufacturer replaced 212 rollers in September.
Interim measure
Spare rollers and bands held on site; failures replaced in the same shift.
StageStatusOwnerTargetCompleteFinding · measure · notes
InvestigationCompleteD. Grigg—10 Nov 2026Failed rollers stripped and examined on site on 10 Nov with Orion's long-term roller manufacturer (AED Rollers) present. Sectioned rollers recorded in ORI-APC-RCA-003.
Root causeCompleteD. Grigg / AED Rollers—10 Nov 2026Rollers supplied on 11 mm hex spring-loaded shafts. The spring loading allows 4 to 6 mm of lateral movement in the frame, so the roller end and the band work against each other: the end pulls out and the band jumps.
DesignCompleteD. Grigg—12 Nov 2026Orion standard arrangement: M8 female-threaded roller ends bolted through the frame on both sides, so the roller cannot move laterally. A new end insert converts the roller to the threaded form.
ImplementationNot startedOrion site teamTo be confirmed—All rollers replaced, skew conveyor first. Lead time about two weeks from order; order placed on receipt of AED Rollers' lead time and price. Changeover by zone, agreed with APC.
VerificationNot startedOrion / APC——Checked in service, run-in, then signed off by APC.

Requirement 1

Nut-and-bolt inspection. Every conveyor, every bolt.

In progressTommy, Orion site supervisor · from Mon 16 Nov until complete

Triggered by the belt tensioners found loosening on 11 November. Tommy is on site from Monday 16 November until the inspection is complete, with C. Morgan for the roller and chute items.

ElementWhat is done
ScopeEvery conveyor section, drive station, roller, bearing, bracket, guide and guard supplied by the original manufacturer.
TighteningEvery fastener checked and brought to torque. Fasteners found loose, missing or wrong are recorded before they are touched.
Match marksA match mark across every bolt and its seat, so any later movement is visible at a glance on every subsequent check. The marks make any later movement obvious at the handover checks.
RecordEach finding recorded with location, measurement, photograph before and after, action taken and the fault it belongs to. Removed parts bagged, tagged and retained.
ReportA written report per conveyor giving its condition at the inspection date. Together the reports are the baseline status of the line and are issued to APC with the next revision of this plan. New faults found are added to the board and numbered on from F-09.
