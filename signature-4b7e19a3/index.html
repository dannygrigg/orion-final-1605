Step 03

Put it in your email

  1. Press Copy signature above.
  2. Turn dark mode OFF first. In Outlook, click the gear icon (top right) → General → Appearance and switch Dark mode off. (If you skip this, Outlook flips the colours and the signature saves white.)
  3. Still in Settings, go to Account → Signatures.
  4. Click + New signature and name it Orion.
  5. Click inside the big empty box and press Ctrl + V (⌘ + V on a Mac).
  6. Under Select default signatures, choose Orion for For new messages and for For replies/forwards.
  7. Click Save. Now you can switch dark mode back on. The signature stays dark.
  8. Send a test email to yourself and click the links in the email you receive.
  1. Press Copy signature above.
  2. Turn dark mode OFF first: File → Office Account → Office Theme, choose White or Colorful. You can switch back after saving.
  3. Click File → Options → Mail, then the Signatures… button.
  4. Click New, type Orion, click OK.
  5. Click in the Edit signature box and press Ctrl + V.

  7. Click OK, then OK again. Send a test email to yourself.

Signature came out white? Outlook was in dark mode when you pasted. Delete it, turn dark mode off, paste again, save.

Links won't open when you click them? While you're writing an email (or in the signature editor), Outlook needs Ctrl + click. The person you send it to just clicks once.
